Curve Converter → Joystick Gremlin

Design in 0-100, read out the -1 … 1 numbers to type into Gremlin. Conversion is g = v/50 − 1 both axes.

Experiment — what this does to the car

Modelled, not measured. The game's own throttle curve is a guess with roughly the right shape — flat for the first third, then exponential. The chain is pedal → your Gremlin curve → the game's curve → throttle, so this is what comes out the far end. Treat the shapes as the argument, not the numbers.
